Accepted Official Solution Verified Answer

Dear Manish, here is the approved academic fees structure for the B.Tech CSE program for the 2026-27 intake batch. Please review the detailed installment schedule below:

Fee Head Frequency Amount (INR)
Tuition Fee Per Semester ₹95,000
University Examination Fee Per Year ₹12,000
Refundable Caution Deposit One-time ₹10,000
Student Activity Charges Per Semester ₹6,500
Total Admission Fee (Semester 1) First Term ₹1,23,500
Important Deadline Note

Installment payments are divided into 2 terms: Term 1 due by July 15, 2026, and Term 2 due by Dec 15, 2026. Late fees accrue at ₹100 per day after the grace period.
